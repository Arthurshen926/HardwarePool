# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in HardwarePool a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and precise.
- Critique designs and evidence, not people.
- Disclose uncertainty and test limitations.
- Respect privacy, especially around audio recordings and device logs.
- Do not pressure contributors to weaken security or bypass platform protections.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, deliberate exposure of private data, deceptive test claims, malicious code, and instructions that endanger contributor systems are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Until a public repository and maintainer team are established, the project owner is responsible for moderation and enforcement. This file should later be replaced or completed with project-specific contact information and an enforcement process.
